CCSellipi is a monoline, handwritten Sinhala display typeface by Damith Welikala (Ceylon Calligraphy), made in 2019. Its letters are written with a single, even pen line, loose and informal, for posters, packaging, signage and titles where a friendly hand-written voice is wanted.

The font was first made in the FM Abhaya ASCII layout, the encoding most Sinhala fonts used before Unicode: Sinhala letters were drawn on Latin keys, so text only looked like Sinhala in that one font. In 2026 Mooniak ported it to Unicode. Every glyph now sits on its Sinhala code point or is reached through OpenType rules, the vowel signs that were typed as separate keys are composed, and the signs are positioned exactly where the original drew them.

The font covers the Sinhala letters in common use with their vowel signs, the repaya, rakaransaya and yansaya, ක්‍ෂ, න්‍ද, ත්‍ව and ද්‍ර, a set of consonant + vowel-sign ligatures, plus digits and basic punctuation, in a single Regular weight.

To contribute, see github.com/mooniak/ccsellipi-font.
